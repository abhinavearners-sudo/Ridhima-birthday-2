A Letter For My Ridhima

Everything I sometimes fail to say ❤️

To my Pyari Chudail, Madam Ji, Malkin, Babyyy, Meri Pagluuu ... and my favourite person, Ridhima. ❤️

Pata nahi main ye sab words mein kitna achhe se likh paunga, but aaj main dil ki woh saari baatein tumse kehna chahta hoon jo shayad main saamne kabhi properly nahi keh pata.

16 November 2025 — hamari first chat hui thi. Tab mujhe bilkul nahi pata tha ki ek normal si conversation meri zindagi ki sabse beautiful story ki beginning ban jayegi.

Phir aaya 18 November 2025... wahi din jab hum pehli baar mile the.

Hum dono us din ekdum anjaan the, lekin mujhe kya pata tha ki jis ladki se main us din mil raha hoon, wahi ek din meri life ka itna important part ban jayegi.

Tumne mujhe chocolate di aur maine tumhe bhi chocolate di. Aur usi din maine tumhe propose kiya... aur tumne bhi mujhe call par propose kiya. ❤️

"Us din main sirf tumse nahi mila tha... us din main apni future wife aur life partner se mila tha." ❤️♾️

Phir 24 December 2025, Dhanush Yagya Mela mein hamari second meeting hui. Tumne mujhe woh rubber band diya tha, jo maine pehna tha.

Chhoti si cheez thi, lekin mere liye woh bahut special thi.

Phir 13 February 2026... Tumne mujhe beautiful watch aur special hankey diya, aur maine tumhe woh cute sa chhota doll gift kiya.

Aur phir 20 February 2026... Ballians Restaurant mein meri advanced birthday party. Tum mere saamne baithi thi aur mera heartbeat literally bahut fast ho raha tha. 😂❤️

Maine tumhe handwritten letter, chocolate aur ring di thi. Shayad us din mujhe aur strongly feel hua ki tum mere liye sirf koi normal person nahi ho.

Ridhima, tumne meri har chhoti se chhoti baat suni hai. Meri bakbak suni hai, meri stupid baatein suni hain, meri khushi mein khush hui ho aur meri problems mein mere saath rahi ho.

Main maanta hoon main perfect nahi hoon. Main kabhi-kabhi bahut zyada bol deta hoon, kabhi mazaak mein limit cross kar deta hoon, kabhi stupid mistakes kar deta hoon...

"But ek cheez kabhi fake nahi thi — Mera pyaar tumhare liye." ❤️

Tumhari smile mujhe genuinely khush karti hai. Tumhari care mujhe special feel karati hai. Tumhara gussa bhi apna sa lagta hai. 😂 Aur tumhari woh chhoti-chhoti pagalpanti... wahi toh meri favourite hai.

Tum simple ho, aur shayad isi simplicity ki wajah se mujhe tum itni beautiful lagti ho.

Main tumhari sirf aaj ki beauty se pyaar nahi karta. Main tumse tumhari poori personality, tumhari aadaton, tumhari smile, tumhari imperfections aur tumhare pure heart ke saath pyaar karta hoon.

Kal tum badal jao, waqt badal jaaye, hum bade ho jaayein, tumhare face par wrinkles aa jaayein, kuch bhi badal jaaye...

"Mera pyaar tumhare liye nahi badlega." ❤️

Aur haan, hamara secret bhi toh hai...

Main chahta hoon ki hum sirf birthdays nahi, bahut saare birthdays saath celebrate karein. Sirf months nahi, bahut saare years complete karein. Sirf achhe moments nahi, mushkil moments mein bhi ek-doosre ka haath pakde rahein.

Aur agar kabhi hum dono mein problems aayein, toh hum ek-doosre se door jaane ke bajay ek-doosre ke paas baithkar solve karein.
